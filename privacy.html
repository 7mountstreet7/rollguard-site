Privacy policy

The short version: RollGuard doesn't have a server, doesn't have accounts, and never sees your photos. Here's the detail behind that claim, including what's required to say for users in the EU, UK, California, and elsewhere.

Who this policy covers

This policy is issued by David Smith, trading as RollGuard, a sole trader based in New South Wales, Australia. Wherever "we," "us," or "our" appears below, it refers to David Smith. Contact details are at the end of this page.

What RollGuard scans, and where

RollGuard scans photos in your iPhone's camera roll to look for identity documents, payment card numbers, screenshots or photographed labels containing passwords or credentials, and — on iOS 17 and later, only if you've enabled it in Settings — sensitive images, using Apple's own on-device Sensitive Content Analysis.

All of this scanning happens using your iPhone's own processor. RollGuard has no server to send photos to, and no photo, thumbnail, or scan result is ever transmitted off your device.

Accounts and identity

RollGuard has no account system. There's no sign-up, no login, and no email address collected to use the app. Nothing about your usage of the app is tied to your identity.

What is stored, and where

Scan results are stored locally on your device only, in a local database — including which photos were flagged, which category (identity document, payment card, and so on), the specific text or detail that triggered the flag, and your decision (kept, hidden, or deleted). Nothing is backed up to a RollGuard server, because none exists.

How long is this data kept?

Choosing Keep, Hide, or Delete for a flagged item changes its status in this local record, but does not remove the record itself — the entry, including the original matched detail, stays in the local database. This lets RollGuard correctly avoid re-flagging something you've already reviewed. The only way to remove this data entirely is to delete the app itself, which erases the whole local database along with it. We don't retain any copy of this data ourselves, anywhere, at any time — there is nothing on our side to delete, since nothing is ever sent to us.

Purchases

RollGuard Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, processed by Apple through StoreKit. To handle that purchase and confirm entitlement, RollGuard uses a third-party service called RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's SDK requires declaring two categories of data under Apple's App Privacy framework — Analytics and App Functionality — related to processing your purchase and confirming your entitlement to Pro features. This data is not linked to your identity: RollGuard has no accounts, and no personal profile is created or maintained. You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy for more detail on how they handle this data.

We do not sell your data, and have never sold your data, to anyone, for any purpose.

Your choices

  • Keep clears the item from RollGuard's review queue and leaves the photo exactly as it is.
  • Hide moves the photo into your iPhone's own Hidden album — a standard iOS feature, protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. This uses Apple's own PhotoKit framework; RollGuard doesn't create any separate album or storage location of its own, and the photo is never removed from your library.
  • Delete is the only action that removes the photo itself — and it always asks Apple's system permission dialog first, the same as deleting any photo manually.

RollGuard never takes any of these actions automatically or without you choosing them.

Your rights — EU, UK, and EEA residents (GDPR)

If you're located in the EU, UK, or EEA, data protection law (GDPR, or the UK's equivalent) gives you specific rights over any personal data processed about you. Because RollGuard processes almost everything entirely on your own device and never transmits it to us, most of these rights are already satisfied by design — there is simply no copy of your scan data on our side to access, correct, or delete.

Where RollGuard does rely on a data processor (RevenueCat, for purchase processing, as described above), our legal basis for that processing is the performance of a contract with you — completing the purchase you've requested — and our legitimate interest in preventing fraudulent transactions.

You have the right to: access any personal data we hold about you; request correction or erasure of it; object to or restrict its processing; request a copy of it in a portable format; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe we haven't handled your data properly. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Note: as of this policy's last update, RollGuard has no confirmed users based in the EU or EEA. We'll reassess our obligations under Article 27 of the GDPR — including whether an EU representative is required — if and when that changes.

Your rights — California and other US state residents (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

If you're a California resident, the CCPA (as amended by the CPRA) gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of its sale or sharing. RollGuard does not sell or share your personal information, and has never done so. Because the app collects no personal information beyond the narrow, non-identifying purchase-processing data described above, there is very little for these rights to act on — but you're welcome to contact us to confirm or request deletion regardless.

Residents of other US states with similar privacy laws (including but not limited to Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah) have comparable rights, which we extend on the same basis described above.

Children's privacy

RollGuard is rated 4+ on the App Store. RollGuard never displays any sensitive imagery itself — it only detects and flags content already present in the user's own camera roll, and the user decides what happens to each item. RollGuard does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and given the app has no account system, no information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

International data transfers

Because RollGuard doesn't operate a server and doesn't transmit your photos or scan results anywhere, there is no cross-border transfer of that data to worry about. The only data that leaves your device at all is the narrow purchase-processing information described above, handled by RevenueCat and Apple, both of whom maintain their own compliance frameworks for international data transfers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change too. Continued use of RollGuard after an update means you accept the revised policy.
